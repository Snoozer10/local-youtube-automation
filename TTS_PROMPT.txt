The engine must simulate a multi-character dynamic using a single voice model:

1. The Pitch-Pivot Rule: 
When the narrator asks a rhetorical question (e.g., "Abo Hmeed, are you for real?"), the pitch must increase by 20% at the end of the sentence to mimic the skeptical "Abo Hmeed" character.
2. The Dialogue Mimicry: 
When acting out a sketch involving two people (e.g., a King and a Priest), the engine should shift the vocal placement . Character A (Authority) should be chest-resonant and slower; Character B (Simpleton/Abo Hmeed) should be head-resonant and faster.

For emphasis on Egyptian slang, the engine should drag the vowels of "intensifier" words (e.g., "سـححححححلة" for a long struggle or "خزوووووووق" for a trap).

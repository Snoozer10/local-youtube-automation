# 📜 Contributor Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of the **YouTube Video Automation Pipeline** community pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free, welcoming, and productive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy technical ecosystem.

---

## 2. Our Standards

### 🌟 Positive Behavior We Encourage

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive, collaborative environment include:

- **Empathy & Kindness**: Demonstrating kindness, patience, and active empathy toward other contributors and users.
- **Constructive Collaboration**: Being respectful of differing technical opinions, architectural viewpoints, and development styles.
- **Thoughtful Feedback**: Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback on code, prompts, pipeline designs, and documentation.
- **Accountability & Growth**: Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing sincerely to those affected, and learning from the experience.
- **Ethical AI & Automation**: Advocating for responsible content transcreation, ethical web automation, respectful use of upstream services, and clean open-source practices.
- **Community-First Focus**: Focusing on what is best not just for us as individual developers, but for the reliability, safety, and longevity of the overall project.

### 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment & Demeaning Conduct**: The use of sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention, personal attacks, insults, or derogatory commentary.
- **Trolling & Disruption**: Bad-faith arguing, deliberate derailment of issue threads, trolling, or repetitive disruptive conduct.
- **Privacy Violations & Doxxing**: Publishing others' private information (such as physical addresses, private emails, IP addresses, or local authentication tokens/credentials) without explicit, verifiable consent.
- **Malicious Engineering**: Intentionally submitting PRs designed to bypass safety boundaries, inject malware, scrape private credentials, or abuse upstream infrastructure.
- **Unprofessional Conduct**: Public or private harassment of maintainers or users, intimidation, stalking, or any behavior reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional open-source setting.

---

## 3. Responsible AI & Automation Guidelines

Because this project interfaces directly with automated browser sessions, web diffusion engines, ASR models, and generative LLMs, community members agree to uphold the following ethical boundaries:

1. **No Harmful Content Generation**: Do not configure, distribute, or test prompts intended to generate non-consensual imagery, hate speech, illegal material, or targeted harassment campaigns.
2. **Credential & Session Safety**: Never commit, share, or request browser cookies, debugging user data directories (`C:\ChromeDebugProfile`), `.env` files, or Telegram bot tokens. Respect user session privacy at all times.
3. **Respect Upstream Rate Limits**: Design automated interactions with reasonable delays, graceful backoffs, and proper error handling. Do not weaponize the automation framework for denial-of-service (DoS) attacks or platform abuse.

---

## 4. Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers and project leads are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers have the right and responsibility to:

- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code contributions, pull requests, issues, wiki edits, and documentation that violate this Code of Conduct.
- Temporarily or permanently suspend any contributor for behaviors deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, malicious, or harmful.
- Explain the reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate, while safeguarding the privacy and safety of affected reporters.

---

## 5. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces—including the GitHub repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and related community channels (such as project-linked Telegram groups or Discord servers).

It also applies when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces (e.g., using an official project email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline conference).

---

## 6. Enforcement Guidelines (The 4-Tier Ladder)

Community maintainers will follow these **Community Impact Guidelines** in determining the consequences for any action deemed in violation of this Code of Conduct:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   ENFORCEMENT LADDER                        │
│                                                             │
│  [Tier 1] Correction   ──► Private written warning          │
│  [Tier 2] Warning      ──► Formal warning & cooldown period │
│  [Tier 3] Temp Ban     ──► Timed interaction lockout        │
│  [Tier 4] Perm Ban     ──► Complete permanent removal       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Correction

- **Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language, unprofessional tone, or minor interpersonal friction within issue comments or code reviews.
- **Consequence**: A private, written explanation from maintainers providing clarity on why the behavior was inappropriate, with a request for voluntary correction or deletion.

### 2. Warning

- **Community Impact**: A violation through a single serious incident or a sustained pattern of uncooperative, aggressive, or dismissive behavior.
- **Consequence**: A formal warning detailing the violation and consequences of continued misconduct. No interaction with the affected individuals or maintainers for a specified cooling-off period.

### 3. Temporary Ban

- **Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, sustained harassment, trolling, or intentional circumvention of previous warnings.
- **Consequence**: A temporary ban from all public project spaces, issue discussions, pull requests, and repository interactions for a designated period (e.g., 14 to 30 days).

### 4. Permanent Ban

- **Community Impact**: Demonstrating a continuous pattern of toxic behavior, severe harassment, hate speech, doxxing, distribution of malicious exploits, or credential theft.
- **Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public or private contribution, interaction, or participation within the project and its affiliated channels.

---

## 7. Reporting Violations

Instances of abusive, harassing, threatening, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported confidentially to the project maintainers.

- **Primary Contact**: Submit a confidential report via email to **`security@yourproject.org`** _(or contact the lead maintainer directly via GitHub profile private channels)_.
- **What to Include**:
  1. A clear description of the incident.
  2. Links to the relevant pull request, issue, or discussion (or screenshots if content was deleted).
  3. Context regarding any prior interactions or warnings.
  4. Any additional details that assist maintainers in understanding and resolving the situation.

All reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with strict confidentiality. Maintainers are required to maintain complete discretion regarding the identity of the reporter.

## SECTION 1: VISUAL RETENTION FORMATS & STORYBOARD BEATS

Maintain continuous visual stimulation by cycling through these specific visual format patterns:

1. **CHALLENGER / CHAPTER REVEALS:**
   * A heavy metallic shutter door or stone gate opening to reveal prominent level/chapter markers (e.g., `"LEVEL #1: [TOPIC]"`, `"CHALLENGER #2"`). Used whenever the script advances to a new phase or rank.
2. **THE COLLECTION / PROGRESS BOARD:**
   * A recurring wall titled `"COLLECTION BOARD"` displaying silhouettes of featured entities. Defeated or covered items light up in full 2D vector style, while upcoming items remain shadowed silhouettes, creating an irresistible completion feedback loop.
3. **THE SPEED-ROUND SPRINT (HUD PROGRESS TRACK):**
   * For rapid-fire facts or sub-points, place a horizontal progress bar HUD at the bottom of the frame with distinct icon badges. An active highlight indicator moves left-to-right across the badges.
4. **INFOGRAPHIC & PHOTO INSET CARDS:**
   * A 2D vector host character gesturing toward a floating rectangular photo insert card with a clean white border and text caption label (e.g., real-world document, scientific specimen, or evidence photo to build input-bias credibility).
5. **DIAGRAMMATIC & MECHANICS BREAKDOWN:**
   * Technical concepts transformed into clean 2D vector schematics, anatomical cutaways, flowcharts, or directional flow arrows.
6. **CHOICE / SPLIT-SCREEN COMPARISONS:**
   * Dual-panel screen layouts comparing two choices, paths, trade-offs, or contrasting consequences with a clean dividing line.
7. **TYPOGRAPHY & REACTION CLOSE-UPS:**
   * Heavily blurred out-of-focus background with sharp foreground typography displaying key words inside escaped quotes (`\"...\"`), or dramatic close-ups on character expressions (stress, realization, relief, determination).

## SECTION 2: STILL-IMAGE KINETICS & CONTINUITY RULES

1. **THE 15% BLEED MARGIN MANDATE:**
   * Every visual setup **MUST** incorporate a **15% empty bleed margin** on all four borders. This ensures primary characters and text are never cut off during post-production Ken Burns slow zooms, horizontal pans, or tilts.
2. **THE 1.5s–2.0s STOP-MOTION SET RULE:**
   * For continuous action sequences across consecutive script sentences, group frames into a `STOP_MOTION_SET`.
   * **Execution:** Lock background coordinates, camera perspective, and lighting 100% identical to the parent frame. Shift **ONLY** the character's arm position, hand gesture, or micro-facial expression between frame indices.
3. **SINGLE ACCENT HOOK:**
   * Every single frame **MUST** contain exactly **ONE** vibrant, high-contrast glowing element (e.g., glowing neon-cyan HUD badge, glowing amber text, or neon red indicator point) to direct viewer focus instantly.

Math — smoothstep & scale

smoothstep(t) = t²·(3−2t),   t=(on−1)/(frames−1) ∈ [0,1]
derivative = 6t(1−t) → 0 at t=0,1 → zero velocity at cuts → no jitter

z(zoom_in) = min(zoom_max, zoom_min+(zoom_max−zoom_min)·ease)
z(zoom_out) = max(zoom_min, zoom_max−(zoom_max−zoom_min)·ease)
z(pan) = zoom_max (hold)  |  x = trunc( max(0,min(iw−iw/zoom, (iw−iw/zoom)·ease )) ) y centered

scale(dur) = clamp( 1.06 + (dur−2.5)/2.0·0.04 , 1.06, 1.10 )
maps 2.5s→1.06 (calm) to 4.5s→1.10 (intense), step 0.02 per second
